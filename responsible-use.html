Responsible use

Last updated 16 September 2026. This page is an interim safeguard for the prototype.

Adults only

ZeePredict content is intended only for people aged 18 or older and for people who may lawfully access gambling-related information where they live.

No guarantee

Sport is uncertain. Analysis, probabilities and odds can be wrong. No prediction should be treated as a guarantee, financial advice or a reason to gamble.

Protect yourself

  • Set strict time and money limits before you start.
  • Never borrow money to bet or use essential household funds.
  • Do not chase losses or increase stakes to recover money.
  • Take regular breaks and stop if gambling causes stress, secrecy or conflict.
  • If you need help, stop gambling and contact a qualified health professional or recognised support service in your location.

Product controls before launch

Commercial launch is blocked until age controls, jurisdiction checks, self-exclusion links, safer-use prompts and reviewed support contacts are implemented.
